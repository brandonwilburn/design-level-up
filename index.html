Five reference sites,
five different feels.

A study in how a single design system — asymmetric layout, typographic depth, real specificity in copy — can hold five very different niches, each with its own palette, voice, and rhythm. Click into any one to see the full page.

The principle that doesn't change.

Each demo above is structurally the same: an asymmetric hero, a long-form pitch with drop cap and pull quote, a product or catalog section, a journal of editorial pieces, and a Visit section with a real Google Maps embed. What changes is the palette, the typography, the voice, and the decorative detail. Same depth, different feels.

Each card links to the full page. Open them in five browser tabs and tab back and forth — the principle is the same, the vibe is the work.
