# Image Brief — Yard & Larder

> What each image slot in `reference/yard-and-larder/` is for, and
> what to look for when sourcing a real photograph. The current
> `img/` folder holds Unsplash placeholders; swap them out for
> real shots as they become available.

The Yard & Larder demo is set in a fictional general store on
2nd Avenue North, Nashville. All photography should be consistent
with that world: warm, real, slightly worn-in, made by people
we'd want to know.

## Visual rules (apply to every image)

- **Light.** Natural, directional, warm. Side-light or window-light.
  No overhead fluorescents. No on-camera flash.
- **Background.** Wood, stone, paper, linen. Never seamless
  white. Never a sweep.
- **Composition.** Off-center, with negative space. Most product
  shots are 3/4 angle or top-down, never dead-on frontal.
- **Color.** Earth tones — cream, oat, amber, oxblood, soft brown.
  No neon. No purple. No tech-product highlights.
- **People.** If a person is in the frame, they should be doing
  something — pouring, cutting, handing, holding. Never posing.
  Never smiling at the camera.
- **Resolution.** 1200px on the long edge is plenty. The page
  caps display at 800px. Higher res is wasteful.
- **Format.** `.jpg` at quality 80. Avoid `.png` for photos
  (use `.png` only for line art / SVG-style illustrations).

## Slots

### Hero — `img/hero-storefront.jpg`

- **What:** The shop exterior, on a downtown street. Ideally
  with the shop's painted sign, a window display, and a hint
  of the neighborhood.
- **Aspect:** 5:6 portrait (the page sets `aspect-ratio: 5/6`).
- **Search terms:** "small independent shop storefront",
  "boutique exterior downtown", "storefront sign painted".
- **Look for:** A shop with character — a hand-painted sign,
  a window display, a brick facade, a sidewalk. Avoid
  generic mall-storefront. Avoid buildings that read as
  corporate.
- **Avoid:** Stock photos of "shops" with mannequins in the
  window. Stock photos with people in crosswalk poses.

### Product photos (1:1 aspect, displayed in a 4:3 box)

The page sets each product art box to a 4:3 aspect ratio. The
`shelf-item--feature` variant uses 1:1.

#### `img/candle.jpg`
- **What:** A pair of beeswax taper candles, hand-rolled,
  on a warm wooden surface. Optional: a wick, a wick trimmer,
  a sheet of beeswax.
- **Search terms:** "beeswax taper candle pair", "handmade
  taper candle", "natural beeswax candle".
- **Look for:** Off-white or warm cream color. Visible texture
  from hand-rolling. Soft side-light.
- **Avoid:** Machine-made candles with smooth plastic-looking
  surfaces. Bright color candles (pink, blue, etc.).

#### `img/coffee.jpg`
- **What:** A bag or tin of freshly roasted coffee, with a few
  beans scattered. Optional: a pour-over setup, a scoop, a
  Chemex.
- **Search terms:** "artisan coffee bag", "freshly roasted
  coffee beans", "specialty coffee tin".
- **Look for:** Kraft paper bag or simple tin. Whole beans
  visible. Warm wood or stone background.
- **Avoid:** Branded coffee (Starbucks-style). Espresso
  machine porn. Cafe interior with people.

#### `img/soap.jpg`
- **What:** A single bar of natural soap, hand-cut, on a stone
  or wood surface. Optional: a soap dish, a sprig of dried
  herb, a few more bars stacked.
- **Search terms:** "handmade bar soap", "natural castile soap",
  "olive oil soap bar".
- **Look for:** Visible hand-cut edges. Cream or oat color.
  Maybe a slight marbling or swirl from the cure.
- **Avoid:** Brightly colored novelty soaps. Liquid soap.
  Bath bombs.

#### `img/skillet.jpg`
- **What:** A well-seasoned cast iron skillet, photographed
  from above. Optional: a wooden spatula, a folded linen
  towel nearby.
- **Search terms:** "cast iron skillet vintage", "seasoned
  cast iron pan", "lodge cast iron".
- **Look for:** Dark patina, well-used. Visible seasoning
  (slight sheen). On a wood counter or open flame background.
- **Avoid:** Brand-new shiny cast iron (looks fake). Non-stick
  pans. Stainless cookware.

#### `img/towel.jpg`
- **What:** A folded linen or cotton kitchen towel, neutral
  color. Optional: a stack of 2-3 towels, a hanging rail.
- **Search terms:** "linen kitchen towel", "cotton tea towel
  folded", "natural kitchen linen".
- **Look for:** Cream, oat, or natural undyed color. Visible
  weave texture. Slight wrinkles (real fabric, not ironed).
- **Avoid:** Bright prints. Plaid. Christmas-themed.

#### `img/okra.jpg`
- **What:** A mason jar of pickled okra, full and colorful.
  Optional: fresh okra pods, a few garlic cloves, a sprig
  of dill.
- **Search terms:** "pickled okra jar", "home canned
  vegetables", "mason jar preserves".
- **Look for:** Brine visible. Whole okra pods. Mason jar
  with a simple lid (no decorative print).
- **Avoid:** Brand-name commercial preserves. Bright plastic
  lids.

#### `img/wraps.jpg`
- **What:** A set of beeswax food wraps, in muted colors,
  laid out or folded.
- **Search terms:** "beeswax wrap set", "reusable food wrap",
  "eco kitchen wrap".
- **Look for:** Muted colors (not neon). Visible cotton
  texture. Set of 2-3, not a single wrap.
- **Avoid:** Plastic wrap. Cling film. Foil.

#### `img/honey.jpg`
- **What:** A jar of raw comb honey, with the comb visible
  through the glass. Optional: a wooden honey dipper.
- **Search terms:** "raw comb honey jar", "honeycomb jar
  hex section", "amber honey jar".
- **Look for:** Visible comb inside the jar (the hex pattern).
  Amber or dark amber color. Glass jar, no plastic.
- **Look for:** Claw jars or hex jars. Wood dipper nearby.
- **Avoid:** Squeezy bear honey bottles. Honey in plastic
  containers.

#### `img/lavender.jpg`
- **What:** A small hand-poured votive candle in a glass
  votive. Optional: a few sprigs of dried lavender, a wick
  trimmer.
- **Search terms:** "small votive candle lavender", "hand
  poured candle glass", "natural votive candle".
- **Look for:** Glass votive (not tin). Cream or pale wax.
  A subtle lavender color if dyed. Soft warm light.
- **Avoid:** Birthday candles. Taper candles. Mass-produced
  Yankee Candle-style products.

## What NOT to use

- **Stock photos that look like stock.** If a photo has the
  dead-eyed "Unsplash model" stare, it doesn't go here.
- **Brand-forward photography.** If a logo is visible, the
  photo doesn't go here.
- **Generic lifestyle photography.** No laptops, no coffee
  cups with latte art, no "person working in a bright
  apartment" shots.
- **AI-generated imagery.** All photos should be real
  photographs of real objects, ideally shot by the merchant
  or by a local photographer.
- **Heavy filter presets.** No VSCO filter aesthetic. No
  Instagram-style saturation. No dark-mode food photography.

## Sourcing notes

- The current `img/` folder is Unsplash placeholders. Use the
  search terms above to find better matches, or commission
  a real photo shoot with a local photographer.
- For a single coherent set, the best source is a day with
  a photographer in the actual shop. Budget 4 hours; that
  covers all 10 slots plus some B-roll for the journal.
- For a more affordable approach, the merchant can shoot
  their own product photos on a phone. Use a sheet of
  butcher paper on a wood counter; shoot in morning light
  from a side window. Avoid flash.
